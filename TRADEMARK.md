但 **GPL 只处理版权，不授予任何商标权利**（见 GPLv3 第 7(e) 条）。「SwiftSQL」这一名称、应用图标及相关视觉标识，是本项目的标识，其权利由项目作者保留，**不随代码许可一并授予**。

- 出于任何目的使用本软件，包括商业环境

> 请注意：依 GPLv3 第 6 条，**任何形式的分发（含销售）都必须同时向接收者提供完整的对应源代码**，且接收者有权继续自由再分发。

**如果你分发的是修改版，请改用你自己的名称和图标。** 这是自由软件生态的通行做法（例如 Debian 曾将 Firefox 改名为 Iceweasel），既保障了你分发代码的自由，也避免用户把两者混淆。

### Copyright and trademarks are separate

The **source code** of SwiftSQL is licensed under the [GNU GPL v3.0 or later](LICENSE). Anyone is free to use, modify and distribute it.

But **the GPL grants copyright permissions only — it grants no trademark rights** (see GPLv3 section 7(e)). The name "SwiftSQL", the application icon and the associated visual identity are marks of this project, reserved by its author and **not conveyed together with the code licence**.

### What you may do (no permission needed)

- Use the software for any purpose, commercial settings included
- Modify the source and distribute your modified version
- Copy and redistribute the software, **including selling copies for a fee** — a right the GPLv3 grants explicitly. This policy does not restrict it, and could not.
- Refer to SwiftSQL descriptively and truthfully, e.g. "built on SwiftSQL" or "compatible with SwiftSQL"

> Note that under GPLv3 section 6, **any distribution — sale included — must supply the recipient with the complete corresponding source**, and that recipient remains free to redistribute it further.

### What requires prior written permission

The following uses involve this project's marks and **require prior written permission from the author**:

- Selling, reselling or offering paid distribution of software, services or subscriptions under the SwiftSQL name
- Using "SwiftSQL" in your product name, service name, company name or domain name
- Using the SwiftSQL application icon, logo or visual identity to brand your distribution
- Implying in any way that your product or service is official, certified, sponsored or endorsed by SwiftSQL

**If you distribute a modified version, please use your own name and icon.** This is standard practice in the free-software world — Debian once shipped Firefox as Iceweasel for exactly this reason. It preserves your freedom to distribute the code while keeping users from confusing the two.

### Resale and commercial distribution

If you plan to resell SwiftSQL commercially or distribute it at scale, **we would like you to get in touch first, whether or not you use the project's name.**

To be precise about what that means: as far as the code itself goes this is **a request, not an obligation** — the GPLv3 grants you the right to sell copies, and we will not, and cannot, take that back. But the moment the "SwiftSQL" name or icon is involved, the trademark permission above applies and **is required**.

Reaching out is usually better for both sides: we can grant a trademark licence, provide support or custom development, or simply issue a [commercial licence](README.en.md#enterprise-and-commercial-licensing) so the GPLv3's copyleft obligations do not apply to you at all.
